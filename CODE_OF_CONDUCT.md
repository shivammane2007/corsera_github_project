
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project. We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels, and we pledge to make participation a positive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
* Welcoming different opinions, experiences, and perspectives.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, insults, and publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication channels. It also applies when an individual represents the project or its community in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers through the repository's available contact channels. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are responsible for protecting the privacy and safety of those reporting incidents and will handle reports with appropriate confidentiality.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may use the following guidelines when determining consequences for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction:** A private or public warning explaining the inappropriate behavior and why it is unacceptable.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with consequences for continued inappropriate behavior.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary restriction from participating in project activities.
4. **Permanent Removal:** A permanent restriction from participating in the project when serious or repeated violations occur.

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the violation.
